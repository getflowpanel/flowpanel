"@flowpanel/core": patch

`series()` folds rows into one point per hour, day, week or month of a range in the admin's zone. Gaps are filled, each `by` value is a column (`value` without `by`), and `top` folds the rest into `OTHER_KEY`; development warns once past 50,000 rows. `breakdown()` ranks rows into `BarRow`s with each row's share of the real total and, given `previous` rows, its previous value. `presetRange()` gives a preset's bounds in the admin's zone, and `fillDays` now calls `series`: it sums numbers, numeric strings, bigints and booleans as before, reads a zoneless timestamp as UTC rather than in the server's zone, no longer sums `Date` values as epoch milliseconds, and starts a range that begins at the epoch at the first row's day instead of 1970.
`BarRow` takes a `key`. A chart's `y` is optional, `labels` names its series, and `bucket` is documented as the tick format it always was; the chart types now live in `types/widget-charts.ts`. `labels.widget.other` names the folded rest.
The zone helpers (`zoneParts`, `zonedTime`, `shiftCalendarDays`, `shiftCalendarMonths`, `resolvePreset`) and `isEnumDef`/`keyLabel` are exported for `@flowpanel/next` and are not part of the stable API.
`bars()` takes `compare: true` or `compare: { against: "weekAgo" }` to fill each row's `previous` from the compared period.
`Threshold` and `toneFor()` declare once where a number turns bad: `ok`, `warn` or `err` in either direction, and `muted` below a minimum volume. `tones` is accepted on `metric`, `stat` and `bars`, and on the `number`, `percent` and `duration` column formats (the `number` format gains an object form); `cellTone()` returns a cell's tone. `MetricResult` and `StatResult` take a `volume`.
Every widget takes `freshness` (`{ at, warnAfter }`) and `coverage` (`CaptionOptions`), and a card shows one `WidgetCaption` line under its title: expired, then where `maxRange` cut the range, then freshness, then coverage. `labels.widget.expired`, `keptSince` and `dataAsOf` word them.
`tracker()` shows status over time: one row per key and one block per hour or day of a window ending now (default `"24h"`). Blocks are folded from rows by `at`, `by`, `value` and `volume`, toned by `tones`, and summarised by an optional async `summary`; `rows` takes hand-built rows instead. `labels.widget.observations` words a block's count, and a drawer tab's widgets exclude the tracker.
`banner()` is a `list()` with `variant: "banner"`: rows ordered by severity then `count`, a glyph per row, a tint by the worst row, and an `allClear` line when empty. Every list takes `limit` and `more` to fold extra rows into one "N more" line, a row takes `count` and `since`, and a static `more` link is checked at `defineAdmin`. The list types now live in `types/widget-list.ts`; `labels.widget.allClear` and `labels.widget.more` word them.
A dashboard takes `filters`, one select each, read by every widget as `ctx.filters`, a new required `WidgetContext` member that is `{}` off a dashboard. `bars`, `tracker` and `table` take `filter` so a row sets it, and `DashboardLink` takes explicit `filters`. `defineAdmin` rejects an undeclared or duplicate filter key and a link to a filter its dashboard lacks, each with the closest key, and a filter on a record page or in a drawer. `labels.widget.filtered` explains an empty card under a filter.

`breakdown({ keys, top })` never folds a declared key into the `OTHER_KEY` rest, same as `series({ keys, top })`.
